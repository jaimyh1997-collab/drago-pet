# Drago Pet

A tiny pixel dragon that keeps you company in the corner of your screen, made for the Dragonomous clan.

Drago reacts to what happens in game:

| What happens | Drago |
|---|---|
| You level up | Puts on a party hat: "gz! Attack 70" |
| Valuable drop, new collection log slot or pet | Holds up a sparkling purple |
| You die | Cries: "sit..." |
| Your HP is low | Sweats nervously |
| You stand still for a minute | Falls asleep |
| Nothing special | Idles, blinks and bobs |

## Settings
- **Size** – 1x to 4x (32px to 128px)
- **Speech bubbles** – on/off
- Each reaction can be switched on or off, and the low-HP threshold is adjustable.

Hold **Alt** and drag Drago to move him anywhere on screen.

Drop and pet reactions use the game's chat messages, so turn on *Loot drop notifications* and *Collection log notifications* in the in-game settings for those.
